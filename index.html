Activities & Recommendations 🌟

Our personal favourites around Thun & the Bernese Oberland – from mountain adventures to cosy restaurants.

⭐ My Top Favourite

Schilthorn incl. James Bond Brunch

Top Pick 🚡 Cableway ❄️ Winter ☀️ Summer

At 2970 m above sea level, the Schilthorn is the highest peak in the Pre-Alps. Famous as the James Bond filming location – enjoy the iconic 007 brunch at Piz Gloria with a breathtaking 360° panorama over Eiger, Mönch & Jungfrau.

📍 Valley station in Lauterbrunnen · ⏱ Full-day trip
🎟️ Book Now

Niesen – Pyramid of Switzerland

🚡 Cableway ☀️ Summer
⛰️

Take the Niesenbahn funicular to 2362 m and enjoy a spectacular 360° panorama over Lake Thun, the Bernese Alps and the Jura. The world's longest staircase (11,674 steps) is a legendary challenge!

📍 Mülenen, ~20 min by car · ⏱ Half-day trip
🌐 Website & Tickets

Lake Thun Boat Cruise

🚢 Boat ☀️ Summer
⛵

Cruise the turquoise waters of Lake Thun with a backdrop of snow-capped Alps. Stop off at Interlaken, Spiez or the Beatushöhlen caves. A perfect lazy afternoon on the water.

📍 Schifflände Thun · ⏱ 2–6 hours
🌐 Timetable & Tickets

Jungfraujoch – Top of Europe

🚂 Train ❄️ Winter ☀️ Summer
🏔️

Europe's highest railway station at 3454 m. Marvel at the Aletsch Glacier, the longest glacier in the Alps. A once-in-a-lifetime experience only 1.5 hours from Thun.

📍 Depart from Interlaken Ost · ⏱ Full day
🌐 Website & Tickets

Hiking around Lake Thun

☀️ Summer 🥾 Hiking
🥾

Dozens of well-marked hiking trails start right from Thun. Walk the lakeside path to Hilterfingen or climb to the Beatenberg ridge for panoramic views. SwitzerlandMobility has all routes with GPS download.

📍 Starting point: Thun city centre · ⏱ 2–6 hours
🌐 Routes & Maps

Skiing – Adelboden & Lenk

❄️ Winter 🎿 Ski
🎿

The Adelboden-Lenk ski area offers 210 km of pistes for all levels. Host of the legendary Hahnenkamm race, it's one of the top winter destinations in the Bernese Oberland – just 45 min from Thun.

📍 Adelboden, ~45 min by car · ⏱ Full day
🌐 Ski Passes & Info

Thun Castle & Museum

🏰 Culture ☀️ Summer
🏰

The impressive Thun Castle perches above the old town and houses a fascinating museum on the region's history. The views from the towers over Thun and the Alps are breathtaking.

📍 Schlossberg 1, 3600 Thun · ⏱ 1–2 hours
🌐 Opening Hours & Info

Restaurant Schlossberg

🍽️ Swiss cuisine ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
🍴

Stunning restaurant right next to Thun Castle with panoramic views over Lake Thun. Regional Swiss specialities with a modern twist. Don't miss the Cordon Bleu – it's legendary!

📍 Schlossberg 1, 3600 Thun · 💰 Mid-range
🗺️ Open in Maps

Thun Old Town & Shopping

🛍️ Shopping ☀️ Summer
🛍️

Thun's old town is famous for its unique covered walkways on the first floor of buildings – an architectural curiosity! Browse boutiques, bakeries and the lively market square. Saturday market is a must.

📍 Thun Old Town, 5 min walk · ⏱ Anytime
