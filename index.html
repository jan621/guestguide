Activities & Recommendations 🌟

Our personal favourites around Interlaken & the Bernese Oberland – from mountain adventures to cosy restaurants.

⭐ My Top Favourite

Schilthorn incl. James Bond Brunch

Top Pick 🚡 Cableway ❄️ Winter ☀️ Summer

At 2970 m above sea level, the Schilthorn is the highest peak in the Pre-Alps. Famous as a James Bond filming location – enjoy the iconic 007 brunch at Piz Gloria with a breathtaking 360° panorama over Eiger, Mönch & Jungfrau.

📍 Valley station in Lauterbrunnen · ⏱ Full-day trip
🎟️ Book Now

Jungfraujoch – Top of Europe

🚂 Train ❄️ Winter ☀️ Summer
🏔️

Europe's highest railway station at 3454 m. Marvel at the Aletsch Glacier – the longest glacier in the Alps. A once-in-a-lifetime experience departing right from Interlaken Ost.

📍 Depart from Interlaken Ost · ⏱ Full day
🌐 Website & Tickets

Harder Kulm – Top of Interlaken

🚡 Funicular ☀️ Summer ❄️ Winter
⛰️

Take the funicular from Interlaken up to 1322 m for a stunning panorama of both Lake Thun and Lake Brienz with the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau as backdrop. Only 10 min from the apartment!

📍 Interlaken, 10 min walk · ⏱ Half-day trip
🌐 Website & Tickets

Lake Brienz Boat Cruise

🚢 Boat ☀️ Summer
⛵

Cruise the stunning turquoise Lake Brienz with views of waterfalls and the Alps. Stop at the idyllic village of Iseltwald or ride the historic steam boat. A perfect afternoon on the water.

📍 Interlaken Ost pier · ⏱ 2–4 hours
🌐 Timetable & Tickets

Hiking in the Bernese Oberland

☀️ Summer 🥾 Hiking
🥾

Hundreds of well-marked trails start right from Interlaken. Walk the lakeside path to Iseltwald, hike up to Lauterbrunnen valley, or explore the Niederhorn ridge. SwitzerlandMobility has all routes with GPS download.

📍 Starting point: Interlaken · ⏱ 2–6 hours
🌐 Routes & Maps

Skiing – Grindelwald & Wengen

❄️ Winter 🎿 Ski
🎿

The Jungfrau ski region offers 213 km of pistes for all levels, directly accessible from Interlaken. Grindelwald and Wengen are world-famous ski villages just 30–40 min away by train.

📍 Grindelwald / Wengen, ~35 min by train · ⏱ Full day
🌐 Ski Passes & Info

Interlaken Town & Höheweg

🛍️ Shopping ☀️ Summer
🏘️

Stroll along the famous Höheweg promenade with its perfect alpine panorama. Browse Swiss watch & chocolate shops, enjoy a coffee on the Kursaal terrace, or relax in the Höhematte park right in the centre.

📍 Interlaken centre, 5 min walk · ⏱ Anytime
🗺️ Open in Maps

Restaurant El Azteca

🍽️ Mexican ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
